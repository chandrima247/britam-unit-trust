Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you apply.

What is a Unit Trust Fund?+
A Unit Trust pools money from many investors and is managed by professionals across assets like deposits, bonds, and equities.
How do I choose a fund?+
Choose based on your goal and risk profile: Money Market for stability, Income for balance, and Equity for growth.
How do I withdraw my money?+
You can request a withdrawal through Britam. Funds are paid to your registered bank account or mobile money wallet.
Can I withdraw anytime?+
Withdrawals depend on the terms of your selected fund.
Are my investments guaranteed?+
No. Unit trust values can go up or down, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
What are the risks?+
Different funds carry different levels of volatility. Lower-risk funds focus on stability while higher-risk funds target stronger long-term growth.
Why is there a risk questionnaire?+
It helps match you to a fund that fits your goals, time horizon, and risk level.
What should I prepare before I start?+
Keep your ID, personal details, source of funds, bank details, and payment details ready.
